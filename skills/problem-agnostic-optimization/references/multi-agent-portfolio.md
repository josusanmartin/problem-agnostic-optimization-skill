# Multi-Agent Portfolio

Use only when multi-agent mode is explicit and workers can concurrently test distinct mechanisms or independently or adversarially review high-risk claims. Keep it unloaded for solo work or one delegated edit.

## Parallelism Must Buy Overlap

Fix the shared contract and protected parent, then start workers immediately; the coordinator experiments while they run. If coordination costs more than candidate work, collapse to solo.

Give each worker the contract, current baseline, one named parent, one distinct mechanism and prediction, a budget/kill rule, and an output path. Before the first concrete return, share validated facts, not another agent's proposed solution.

Set no fixed worker quota. Duplicate a family only for replication, independent derivation, or a separable implementation.

## Delegate Experiments, Not Narration

Use one compact return and active registry:

```text
family | owner | parent | mechanism/prediction | budget/kill | artifact/proof/screen/blocker | next/reopen
```

Status-only reports earn no follow-up budget. A reduction counts only when it yields an executable candidate, closes measurable search space, or changes the next decision. An unresolved claim equivalent in strength to the original target is `BLOCKED`; its packet names the failed premise and new fact required to reopen it.

## Serialize Promotion, Not Exploration

Workers use the cheapest faithful screen. The coordinator full-checks only plausible survivors, invalidates stale parents, and alone promotes the protected best. Serialize promotion; do not serialize exploration.

Require independent review for a claimed proof or counterexample, a changed contract assumption, or a high-risk winner. Routine executable candidates do not wait for a second agent.

## Reallocate By Yield

After each concrete return, merge duplicates and fund the family with the best expected gain or information per cost. Redirect dry or redundant workers to a distinct family. Stop a worker whose coordination cost exceeds its candidate or information yield.

Cross-pollinate only after evidence. Share validated facts, counterexamples, and resource limits; launch a hybrid only when the transfer removes a named blocker.

When Scorebench is active, derive worker history, attempts, budgets, and promotions there and do not mirror them locally. Otherwise keep coordination in active context unless persistence was requested.
