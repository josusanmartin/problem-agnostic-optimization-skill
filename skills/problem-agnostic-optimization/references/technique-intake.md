# Technique Intake And Breakthrough Mining

Use when outside evidence or a cheaper search tool may expose a mechanism beyond the current hill.

## Intake Interface

Treat an external result as evidence to explain, not an artifact to copy. Retain:

```text
source/provenance | contract and target match | timed lifecycle | observed result
causal mechanism | enabling assumptions | changed resource or invariant | cheapest faithful probe
```

Check common ancestry before treating agreement as independent. Re-derive the mechanism, verify that the transferred route executes, and separate algorithm, implementation, mapping, integration, availability, enforcement, and measurement failures. A regression from an immature port does not close the method.

If a materially faster complete artifact exists, return to the router for frontier introspection. Never use locked source, leaked outputs, hidden-test constants, or harness defects as optimization evidence.

## Breakthrough Mining

Mine prior history only when it can prevent duplicate work or reveal a new hill. Prioritize transitions that changed a resource tier, active floor, phase owner, validation boundary, searchable region, or external frontier.

For a useful transition, extract:

- What changed in the algorithm, dependency graph, representation, primitive, policy, schedule, or search tool.
- The contract or invariant that licensed it.
- The resource saved, resource spent, remaining co-binders, and repair or fallback.
- The evidence that made it searchable and the authority that promoted it.
- Which earlier closures or tuned assumptions became stale.

Do not force discoveries into a fixed tactic catalog. Turn the causal difference into one falsifiable candidate against the current protected best, with a prediction, cheap discriminator, budget, and reopen condition.

Keep this map in active context or the external harness; do not start a logging system for it.

## Calibrate Cheap Screens

Before a screen filters a large search:

- Reproduce representative clean and dirty cases under the same contract.
- Cover scored factors that could create false clean or false rejection.
- State what the screen can reject and what still requires authority.
- Measure combined knobs directly when their errors can interact.

A screen proposes; authority promotes. Downgrade one that repeatedly mispredicts authority and do not let it veto candidates.

## Handle Validation Islands

Contract-allowed selectors, seeds, nonces, routes, or rerolls may change the validation stream without changing required computation. State why the selector is allowed and what it changes. Treat an old clean island as stale after route or operation-order changes, and require the full gate for every selected artifact.
